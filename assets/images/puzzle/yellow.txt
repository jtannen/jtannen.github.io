Bumble is a small, friendly-looking robot resembling a honeybee. Its hexagonal body is made of gold metal with a glossy finish. A small plant sprout with green leaves grows from the top of its head, and two delicate, translucent, veined wings are attached to its back. A flexible, ribbed hose with a nozzle extends from the left side of its body. Its feet are small and round, and its face, with its simple black eyes and mouth, can show a range of emotions, from happy to sad and angry.
