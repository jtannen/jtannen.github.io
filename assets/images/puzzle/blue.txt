Nautilus has a spherical, blue metallic body with six legs, giving it a spider-like appearance. The most striking feature is the transparent, glass-like dome on its front that holds a single, swimming goldfish. The robot has two glowing yellow eyes and two articulated arms with three-fingered claws at the end. Its legs are jointed, and it can move in a variety of ways, including on its four larger legs or with the two front ones lifted in an "alt. movement" stance.
